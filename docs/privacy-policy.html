The Garden – Privacy Policy

The Garden is the community app for UCity The Church. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, used to sign you in and show your name on what you post.
  • What you share: events, volunteer sign-ups, RSVPs, prayer requests, needs and offers, and chat messages (including any photos or files you choose to attach).
  • Reports and blocks you make, so we can keep the community safe.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How it is used

Your information is used only to run The Garden: signing you in, showing posts and events to other members, and delivering your messages. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Other signed-in members can see your name and the things you post. Chat messages are visible only to the people in that conversation.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (authentication and database), which stores account and post data.
  • Stream (getstream.io), which stores and delivers chat messages.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account). This removes your profile, your prayer requests and needs/offers posts, and your chat account. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

The Garden is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete Account. Or email yminor13@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Community Guidelines

The Garden is a place for the UCity The Church family to connect, pray, serve and help one another. By creating an account you agree to these guidelines.

Be kind and respectful

No harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, or personal attacks.

Keep it appropriate

No sexual, violent, or otherwise objectionable content. There is zero tolerance for abusive content or users.

Be honest

No spam, scams, impersonation, or misleading posts. Needs and offers should be genuine.

Respect privacy

Don't share other people's personal information or prayer requests outside the app without their permission.

Reporting and blocking

Use the ••• menu on any post to report it or block its author, and the flag option on chat messages. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; content that breaks these guidelines is removed and the people who posted it may be removed from the app.

Your account

You are responsible for what you post. You can delete your account at any time from your profile.
